01 · The hand

Every roll starts in a palm.

Each piece of dough is weighed, shaped and set down by hand before it ever meets a machine. It is slow on purpose, and it is why no two loaves are quite alike.

02 · The fire

Then the oven takes over.

Steam, heat and a few precise minutes turn soft dough into something with a crackling crust.

03 · The crust

Out of the heat, onto the rack.

Baguettes are lifted out while the crust is still singing. We let them rest just long enough to set the crumb, then send them out.

Good bread should crackle when you hold it.
